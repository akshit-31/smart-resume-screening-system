About

Built for smarter hiring

ResumeIQ is an open-source AI tool that helps recruiters find the best candidates faster — and helps job seekers understand exactly what they need to improve.

Mission

Hiring should be based on skill, not luck

Too many qualified candidates get filtered out by keyword-matching ATS systems that don't understand context. Meanwhile, recruiters spend hours reading resumes manually when the best match could be found in seconds.

We built ResumeIQ to solve both sides of this problem — giving recruiters an AI-powered shortlist and giving candidates transparent, actionable feedback on why they're being passed over.

The Product

What we built

Smart Matching

TF-IDF + cosine similarity gives every resume a precision relevance score against your job description.

AI Roast

Google Gemini provides brutally honest critique and rewrites each resume in context of the job description.

Deep Analysis

ATS scoring, keyword gap analysis, skill extraction, and resume section parsing — all in one view.

AI Optimizer

Generate an improved version of any resume tailored to the specific role — no experience fabrication.

Tech Stack

Built on proven technology

Industry-standard NLP libraries for text processing and matching, combined with Google Gemini for the generative AI features. Runs on a clean Python/Flask web stack deployable to Render or Vercel in minutes.
